Xera's messaging system lets you connect directly with other builders on the platform. Whether you want to give feedback on someone's Trace, propose a collaboration on an ARC, or simply reach out to a builder whose Trajectory impressed you, direct messages give you a private, persistent channel to do it.

## Accessing Your Messages

Click the **Messages** icon in the main navigation bar to open your inbox. A badge on the icon shows the number of unread messages across all your conversations. The badge updates in real time — when a new message arrives, the count increases immediately without requiring a page refresh.

<Note>
  The Messages section is only accessible to logged-in users. If you are not signed in, clicking the Messages icon redirects you to the login page.
</Note>

## Starting a Conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the builder's profile">
    Navigate to the profile of the builder you want to message. You can reach a profile from the discovery feed, search results, a Trace card, or a stream page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Message">
    On their profile page, tap or click the **Message** button. Xera automatically creates a conversation between you and that builder if one does not already exist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your first message">
    Type your message in the composer at the bottom of the chat panel. Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift + Enter** to add a new line. You can also attach an image or video using the **+** button next to the input field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Conversation Features

The messaging interface is split into two panels: a conversation list on the left and the active chat on the right.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Conversation List" icon="list">
    All your active threads appear here, sorted by most recent activity. Each thread shows the other person's name, a message snippet, and the timestamp of the last message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unread Indicators" icon="circle-dot">
    Unread message counts appear as badges on individual threads. Opening a conversation marks all its messages as read automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media Attachments" icon="paperclip">
    Attach images or videos to any message. A preview renders inline before you send, so you can confirm the file looks right.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real-Time Delivery" icon="bolt">
    Messages arrive in real time. If your connection drops, Xera queues outgoing messages and retries delivery automatically when you reconnect.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Sending a Message

Type up to **4,000 characters** per message. For most conversations, keep messages focused and actionable — builders appreciate direct, substantive communication over vague check-ins.

```text
Enter          → Send message
Shift + Enter  → New line (multi-paragraph messages)
+ button       → Attach an image or video
```

### Managing a Conversation

Inside any conversation, the header provides quick access to additional actions:

- **Delete** — hide the conversation from your list. It reappears automatically if the other person sends you a new message
- **Block** — prevent future messages from that user. You can unblock them later from **Account Settings → Privacy**

## Messaging Limits

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Account required | You must be signed in to send or receive messages |
| Self-messaging | You cannot open a conversation with yourself |
| Blocked users | Blocked users cannot message you; you cannot message users who have blocked you |
| Attachment types | Images and videos only |

## Use Cases

<Accordion title="Collaborate on an ARC">
  If you want to work with another builder on a shared goal, reach out via DM to align on scope and milestones before either of you commits publicly. A short message with your ARC idea and what role you envision for them is enough to start a real collaboration conversation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Give feedback on a Trace">
  Leave a public comment on a Trace for visible feedback. Use a direct message for more detailed or private input — for example, if you spotted a bug, have a specific suggestion, or want to share a resource without cluttering the public thread.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Discuss joining an ARC together">
  Some builders post ARCs that could benefit from a contributor. If you see an ARC in a domain where you have relevant skills, message the builder to express genuine interest and explain what you could add.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Message builders whose Trajectory genuinely impresses you. Xera's community values direct, authentic outreach. A message that references a specific Trace or milestone is far more likely to get a response than a generic "let's connect."
</Tip>
