Xera search helps you find builders working in your domain, discover active ARCs, and explore Traces by topic. Whether you have a specific name in mind or you are browsing to see who is making progress in a field you care about, search is the fastest way to move from curiosity to connection. Results are not just keyword matches — they are ranked, filtered, and discoverable.

## What You Can Search

Xera search covers three main content types:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="User Profiles" icon="user">
    Search by name or username to find a specific builder. Results include follower count, verified badge, and a short bio snippet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ARCs by Topic" icon="drafting-compass">
    Search for active projects and goals. ARC titles and descriptions are indexed, so relevant missions surface even if you do not know the builder's name.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Traces by Keyword" icon="file-lines">
    Search the content of Trace logs. Find builders documenting progress in a specific technology, method, or domain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Verified Builders" icon="badge-check">
    Filter results to show only verified builders — creators with an active paid plan and the Xera verified badge.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How Search Results Are Ranked

Xera search does not return results in random order or sorted purely by keyword relevance. The same **composite scoring algorithm** that powers the discovery feed also ranks search results. That means:

- Builders who post consistently score higher than dormant accounts
- Verified builders with engaged audiences surface before inactive profiles
- ARCs with recent Trace activity rank above stale projects
- Creators with higher engagement signals (likes, comments, tips) appear earlier

A builder with a clear, specific profile bio and active ARCs will consistently outrank an equally keyword-matched profile that has not posted in weeks.

<Info>
  Your profile **bio**, **ARC titles**, and **Trace content** are all indexed by the search engine. Write them clearly and specifically — vague bios like "entrepreneur" or "developer" are harder to surface than specific ones like "building an AI scheduling app in Python" or "learning electrical engineering through hands-on projects."
</Info>

## Filtering Results

Use the available filters to narrow your search to exactly the right builders or content:

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Domain / Skill area** | Limit results to builders working in a specific field (e.g., software, design, hardware, fitness) |
| **Activity level** | Show only builders who have posted a Trace recently (active) or within the past month (recent) |
| **Verified builders** | Show only creators with an active paid plan and a verified badge |

Combine filters to get highly specific results — for example, **verified** + **active** + **software** surfaces the most consistent builders in the software domain who are posting today.

## Following Directly from Search

You do not need to open a full profile to follow someone. Tap **Follow** directly on a search result card. The follow is registered immediately, and that builder will start appearing more reliably in your discovery feed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your search term">
    Type a name, topic, or keyword in the search bar. Results appear as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters (optional)">
    Use the filter options to narrow by domain, activity level, or verified status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow from the card">
    Tap **Follow** on any result card. The button updates to **Following** instantly. No page navigation required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore their profile">
    Tap the builder's name or avatar to open their full profile, view their Trajectory, and browse their ARCs and Traces before deciding whether to follow or reach out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discovering Builders Without Searching

Search is intentional — you already have something in mind. For ambient discovery, Xera offers two additional paths:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discovery Feed" icon="rss" href="/features/feed">
    Your main feed surfaces builders ranked by the composite algorithm. Scroll to find people you have never heard of who are doing interesting work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Builders Section" icon="users">
    Browse the dedicated Builders directory to explore active creators sorted by engagement, plan tier, and domain. No search query needed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Your own discoverability depends on how clearly you describe your work. Update your **profile bio** with specific technologies, goals, or domains you are working in. Give your **ARCs** precise, descriptive titles. Write **Trace** content that mentions what you are building and how — these are the signals Xera indexes to surface you in relevant searches.
</Tip>
