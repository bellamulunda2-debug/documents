Xera notifications keep you informed when people engage with your work. Every like on a Trace, new follower, incoming tip, or live stream from a builder you follow generates a notification so you can respond in the moment — not hours later. Notifications are delivered both inside the app and, with your permission, directly to your device via push.

## Notification Types

Xera generates notifications for the following events:

| Notification | When it fires |
|---|---|
| **New follower** | Someone starts following your profile |
| **Trace liked** | A builder likes one of your Traces |
| **Trace commented** | A builder leaves a comment on one of your Traces |
| **New supporter (tip received)** | A viewer sends you a tip during a live stream or via your profile |
| **New live stream** | A builder you follow starts a live stream |
| **ARC milestone reached** | You complete a milestone inside one of your active ARCs |
| **Subscription renewal reminder** | Your subscription plan is approaching its renewal date |

<Info>
  Live stream notifications are sent only to followers of the streamer, and only if the streamer is on an active paid plan (Standard, Medium, or Pro).
</Info>

## Accessing Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the navigation bar to open the notification panel. The bell displays a badge with your current unread count — the number updates in real time as new notifications arrive, so you always know when something needs your attention.

The panel shows your 50 most recent notifications, sorted from newest to oldest. Each entry displays:

- The notification type and a short message
- The actor's avatar (the person who triggered the notification)
- A relative timestamp (e.g., "5 min ago", "2h", "3 days ago")

Clicking any notification opens the relevant content directly — a Trace, a stream, your ARC dashboard, or your subscription settings — and marks the notification as read.

## Marking Notifications as Read

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mark individually">
    Click any notification in the panel. It is automatically marked as read when you open it, and the unread badge count decreases accordingly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mark all as read">
    Open the notification panel. All unread notifications are marked as read as soon as the panel opens — you do not need to click each one individually. The badge clears immediately.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Push Notifications

Push notifications let Xera alert you even when the browser tab is closed or the app is in the background. Enabling push gives you real-time alerts for new messages, live streams from builders you follow, and incoming tips.

### Enabling Push Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Respond to the permission prompt">
    When you first sign in, Xera displays a permission banner at the top of the screen: **"Activer les notifications"**. Click **Autoriser** (Allow) to grant push permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in your browser">
    Your browser will show its own native permission dialog. Click **Allow** to confirm. This is a one-time step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive push alerts">
    Once granted, Xera registers your device via the Web Push API. You will receive notifications even when the app is not open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you dismissed the permission prompt or selected **"Plus tard"** (Later), Xera will not request permission again automatically. To enable push manually, go to your browser's site settings and grant notification permission for the Xera domain, then reload the page.
</Note>

<Warning>
  If your browser shows **Denied** for notifications, Xera cannot override that setting. You must clear the block in your browser's site permissions and reload the page before the prompt will reappear.
</Warning>

### How Push Delivery Works

Xera uses the **Web Push API** with a VAPID key pair to send push messages via your browser's push service. When a qualifying event occurs (new message, new live stream, incoming tip), the Xera server sends a push payload to all registered devices for your account. Your device's service worker intercepts the payload and displays the notification, including a title, body, and an icon — even if the app tab is closed.

Clicking a push notification opens Xera directly to the relevant content.

## Managing Notification Preferences

To control which notification types you receive, navigate to **Account Settings → Notifications**. Each notification type can be toggled on or off independently.

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Toggle **off** | You no longer receive that notification type inside the app or via push |
| Toggle **on** | Notifications resume immediately for future events |

<Tip>
  If you find the live chat notification volume high during active streams, Xera automatically suppresses live chat notifications while you are actively viewing that stream — so you do not get alerted for every chat message while you are already watching.
</Tip>
