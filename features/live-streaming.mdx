Stream your build sessions, demos, and milestone reveals live to your Xera audience. Live streaming on Xera is built for builders — not entertainers. Whether you are debugging a hard problem, shipping a feature, or walking through your ARC progress, going live gives your community a real-time window into your process and a way to support you as you build.

## Starting a Live Stream

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create menu">
    Tap the **+ Create** button in the main navigation bar. Select **Start Live** from the options that appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your stream">
    Enter a **stream title** and an optional **description**. A clear, specific title helps your followers understand what you are building and draws in relevant viewers from the discovery feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow camera and microphone">
    Your browser will request camera and microphone permissions. Grant both to enable a full video and audio stream. You can mute your microphone or disable the camera at any point during the live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Tap **Go Live** to start broadcasting. Your stream is immediately visible to your followers and eligible to appear in the Xera discovery feed. Followers receive a push notification that you are live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Followers receive a live notification only if they have push notifications enabled and you are on an active paid plan (Standard, Medium, or Pro).
</Note>

## Stream Features

Once you are live, the stream interface gives you and your viewers a full set of tools:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Live Video" icon="video">
    Real-time video broadcast at up to HD quality. Pro plan creators can enable HD streaming in their account settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real-Time Chat" icon="comments">
    Viewers send messages up to 500 characters. Chat scrolls automatically and includes sender names and verified badges.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Viewer Count" icon="users">
    A live viewer counter updates every 10–15 seconds and is visible to both you and your audience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tips (Soutenir)" icon="gem">
    Viewers can send tips via the **Soutenir** button. Tips route directly to your creator wallet (requires Medium or Pro plan).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Host Controls

While you are live, a **host controls panel** is available to manage your stream in real time:

- **Mute / unmute microphone** — silence your audio without ending the stream
- **Disable / re-enable camera** — toggle your video feed; the stream continues with audio only when the camera is off
- **Switch camera** — cycle between available video input devices (front/back on mobile, or multiple connected cameras)
- **Share screen** — broadcast your screen with an optional picture-in-picture camera overlay
- **End stream** — confirm and terminate the broadcast

### Audio and Fullscreen Controls

Viewers control their own audio and display independently:

- Toggle audio on or off using the audio button in the player
- Enter fullscreen mode for an immersive viewing experience
- An unmute overlay appears automatically if autoplay is blocked by the browser — tap it to enable audio

## The Viewer Experience

From a viewer's perspective, joining a live stream is immediate:

1. Tap a live stream card from the discovery feed or a notification link
2. The stream loads and begins playing automatically
3. Join the chat — type a message and press **Send**
4. Follow the host directly from the stream page with the **Follow** button
5. Send a tip by tapping **Soutenir** and completing the payment on the checkout screen

Viewers can scroll back through recent chat history to catch up on anything they missed before joining.

## Receiving Tips During a Live Stream

When a viewer sends a tip, the amount is credited to your **creator wallet** after Xera's 20% platform commission. You receive 80% of every tip.

<Warning>
  Receiving tips during live streams requires a **Medium** ($6.00/mo) or **Pro** ($10.00/mo) plan, and your account must have at least 1,000 followers to qualify for the Medium plan. Verify your eligibility in **Account Settings → Subscription**.
</Warning>

## Video Monetization on Recorded Streams

Xera processes your completed stream as a recorded video after you end the broadcast.

<Info>
  Video monetization ($0.40 per 1,000 views) applies to recorded streams that are **longer than 60 seconds** once processing is complete. Streams under 60 seconds do not qualify for view-based revenue. This feature requires an active **Pro** plan.
</Info>

## Tips for a Great Stream

<Accordion title="Announce in advance">
  Post a Trace the day before your stream with a clear title and start time. Traces feed the discovery algorithm — an announcement Trace builds anticipation and surfaces your upcoming stream to new followers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Keep streams focused on building">
  Xera audiences respond to process-driven content. Show your code, your design decisions, your debugging session, your milestone review. Talk through your thinking. Streams that document real work outperform generic "just chatting" sessions on Xera.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Interact with chat">
  Read chat messages aloud and respond to questions. Direct interaction increases dwell time and return visitor rates — both of which feed your recommendation score. A builder who talks with their audience grows faster than one who ignores it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="End with a summary">
  Before going offline, summarize what you built or decided during the stream. Your audience will remember it, and you can log it as a Trace immediately after ending — keeping your momentum visible in your Trajectory.
</Accordion>
