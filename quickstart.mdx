Xera is built for people who build things. In the next five minutes you will create an account, shape your public identity, open your first ARC (a structured goal or project), and publish your first Trace — a proof-of-work update that starts your visible Trajectory on the platform. By the end of this guide, your progression will be live for the world to see.

## Prerequisites

You need only two things before you begin:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- An email address — or a GitHub or Google account for one-click sign-up

<Note>
  Xera is a Progressive Web App (PWA). After you sign in, look for the **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install"** prompt in your browser's address bar to install Xera as a native-feeling app — no app store required.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [xera.app](https://xera.app) and click **Sign Up**.

    Choose your preferred method:

    - **Email** — enter your address and a strong password, then click **Create account**
    - **Google** — click **Continue with Google** and authorise the connection
    - **GitHub** — click **Continue with GitHub** and authorise the connection

    If you signed up with email, Xera sends a verification message to your inbox. Open it and click **Confirm your account** to activate your session. You are then redirected to the onboarding flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Your public profile is the face of your Trajectory. Fill in each field to help other builders find and follow your work.

    | Field | What to enter |
    |---|---|
    | **Username** | A unique handle — this appears in your public URL (`xera.app/@username`) |
    | **Display name** | Your full name or the name you build under |
    | **Bio** | One or two sentences about what you are building and why |
    | **Avatar** | Upload a photo or image that represents you |
    | **Area of focus** | Choose the category that best describes your work: **Developer**, **Creator**, **Entrepreneur**, **Student**, or **Other** |

    Tap **Save profile** when you are done. Your profile is immediately visible to other users on the platform.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first ARC">
    An ARC is a structured goal or project — the container for everything you are building. Think of it as a public mission statement backed by milestones.

    1. Tap the **＋** button in the navigation bar and select **New ARC**.
    2. Give your ARC a clear, specific name (for example: *"Launch my SaaS MVP"* or *"Learn Rust in 90 days"*).
    3. Write a short description explaining the goal and why it matters to you.
    4. Add your first milestones — the key checkpoints on the way to completion.
    5. Tap **Create ARC** to publish it.

    Your ARC is now live on your Trajectory. Every Trace you log from this point will attach to it, building a public record of your execution.

    <Tip>
      Start with three to five milestones. You can add, update, or reorder them at any time as the project evolves.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log your first Trace">
    A Trace is your daily proof of work — a concrete update that shows what you actually did, not just what you plan to do.

    1. Open your ARC and tap **Log a Trace**.
    2. Describe what you worked on today. Be specific: share a decision you made, a problem you solved, or a result you measured.
    3. Optionally attach **images** (screenshots, sketches, photos) or a **video** (recordings, demos, walkthroughs).
    4. Tap **Publish Trace**.

    Your Trace is now part of your public timeline and will appear in the feeds of your followers and the discovery section.

    <Info>
      Video Traces longer than 60 seconds are eligible for video monetization once you reach the Pro plan. Start recording your process now and the content will count toward your earnings later.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the feed">
    Xera's feed surfaces the builders who are showing the most consistent, high-quality progression. Scroll through the **Discover** tab to find creators in your field.

    - **Follow** builders whose Trajectories inspire you
    - **Like and comment** on Traces that resonate
    - **Watch Live Streams** where builders share work in real time and accept tips ("Soutenir")

    The algorithm prioritises verified builders (65 % of your feed) and organic discovery (35 %), weighted by engagement quality, freshness, and builder consistency — so the more active you are, the more visible you become.
  </Step>
</Steps>
