## Why Xera?

Most platforms reward noise — viral moments, polished aesthetics, and engagement bait. Xera rewards proof of progression. Every trace you publish is timestamped evidence of real work, and every ARC you complete adds to a trajectory that speaks louder than any bio.

## Key Features

- **ARC System** — Structure your projects and goals with milestones, progress logs, and a clear narrative arc
- **Trace Logging** — Publish daily proof-of-work updates with text, images, videos, and metrics
- **Trajectory Dashboard** — A visual timeline of your consistency, momentum, and growth over time
- **Discovery Feed** — A recommendation algorithm that surfaces serious builders, not just popular accounts
- **Live Streaming** — Broadcast live to your audience with real-time chat and tip support
- **Creator Monetization** — Earn from supporter tips and video views ($0.40 per 1,000 views)
- **Subscription Plans** — Unlock badges, monetization, and advanced analytics with Standard, Medium, or Pro plans
- **Elite Recruiter Access** — Search certified builder profiles and connect with top talent
- **Mobile Money Payouts** — Withdraw earnings via Airtel Money, Orange Money, M-Pesa, and more
