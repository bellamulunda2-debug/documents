Xera lets you earn real money from the audience you build. Once you meet the eligibility requirements, three distinct revenue streams open up: supporter tips sent directly during live streams and on your profile, video revenue generated by views on long-form content, and the enhanced visibility that comes with a paid subscription plan. Every payment flows through KPay and lands in your creator wallet, ready to withdraw to your Mobile Money account.

## Revenue Streams

## Requirements to Monetize

Before Xera activates monetization on your account, two conditions must both be true.

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Follower count** | At least **1,000 followers** on your Xera profile |
| **Subscription plan** | **Medium ($6.00/mo)** or **Pro ($10.00/mo)** |

<Warning>
  Monetization cannot be activated until your account reaches **1,000 followers**, even if you are already on a Medium or Pro plan. Your plan upgrade takes effect immediately, but the monetization toggle only unlocks once the follower threshold is met.
</Warning>

## Commission Structure

Xera applies a flat **20% platform commission** to every transaction — tips and video payouts alike.

| Revenue type | Gross amount | Xera commission | You receive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supporter tip | Any amount | 20% | **80% net** |
| Video RPM payout | $0.40 / 1k views | 20% | **$0.32 / 1k views net** |

The commission is calculated automatically by the platform. The **net amount** is what gets credited to your available wallet balance.

## Getting Paid

When your available balance reaches the **$5.00 minimum**, you can request a withdrawal to your Mobile Money account. Xera supports the following payout channels:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Airtel Money" icon="mobile">
    Available across multiple African markets. Enter your Airtel number to receive funds directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Orange Money" icon="mobile">
    Widely used in West and Central Africa. Link your Orange Money number in Payout Settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="M-Pesa" icon="mobile">
    East Africa's leading Mobile Money platform. Withdrawals arrive in 2–5 business days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Afrimoney & Other" icon="mobile">
    Afrimoney and other Mobile Money providers are also supported under the "Other" option.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Withdrawals are processed manually by the Xera team. You will receive a push notification when your withdrawal status changes to **Processing** or **Paid**.
</Note>
