Xera offers three paid subscription tiers that unlock progressively more powerful tools for serious builders. Every plan sits on top of the free account and adds features designed to help you grow faster, earn from your audience, and stand out in the feed. Choose the tier that matches where you are in your journey — you can always upgrade or downgrade later.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Standard ($2.50/mo) | Medium ($6.00/mo) | Pro ($10.00/mo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verified blue badge | ✗ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Feed ranking boost | ✗ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Receive tips (Soutenir) | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Video monetization ($0.40/1k views) | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ |
| Advanced analytics dashboard | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ |
| Followers required to activate | — | None | **1,000** | **1,000** |

<Note>
  The Standard plan's verified blue badge increases your composite feed score under the Creator Quality signal, giving your content higher organic reach without requiring any follower threshold.
</Note>

## Plan Details

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standard — $2.50/mo">
    The Standard plan is the entry point for builders who want to establish credibility on Xera without committing to a full monetization setup.

    **What you get:**
    - ✅ Verified blue badge displayed on your profile and across all content
    - ✅ Boosted weight in the feed algorithm's Creator Quality signal (30% of composite score)
    - ✅ Priority consideration in the 65% verified-builder feed slice

    **What's not included:**
    - ✗ Receiving supporter tips
    - ✗ Video monetization
    - ✗ Advanced analytics

    **Follower requirement:** None — activate Standard on any account.

    <Tip>
      Even without direct monetization, the verified badge meaningfully improves your content's reach. It is the fastest way to differentiate your profile in discovery.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Medium — $6.00/mo">
    The Medium plan unlocks the first tier of direct earnings. Once you reach 1,000 followers, Xera activates tip receiving on your profile so your audience can support you financially.

    **What you get (everything in Standard, plus):**
    - ✅ Receive supporter tips ("Soutenir") from any viewer
    - ✅ 80% net on all tips received (Xera retains 20% commission)
    - ✅ Creator wallet to receive, hold, and withdraw earnings to Mobile Money

    **What's not included:**
    - ✗ Video monetization (RPM)
    - ✗ Advanced analytics dashboard

    **Follower requirement:** **1,000 followers** required to activate monetization features.

    <Warning>
      Upgrading to Medium before you reach 1,000 followers is valid — your plan activates immediately and your badge appears right away — but tip receiving only turns on once the follower threshold is met.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Pro — $10.00/mo">
    The Pro plan is designed for creators who publish video content regularly and want full visibility into their performance. It includes everything in Medium and adds video RPM monetization plus an advanced creator dashboard.

    **What you get (everything in Medium, plus):**
    - ✅ Video monetization at **$0.40 per 1,000 eligible views**
    - ✅ Revenue counted only for videos **longer than 60 seconds**
    - ✅ Advanced creator dashboard with detailed video stats, eligible view counts, and estimated earnings
    - ✅ Full transaction history with gross amount, commission breakdown, and net credited

    **Follower requirement:** **1,000 followers** required to activate monetization features.

    <Note>
      Only videos longer than 60 seconds generate view-based revenue. Shorter clips are excluded from RPM calculations even if they receive high view counts.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to your profile and tap the **Settings** icon (gear icon in the top-right corner).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Subscription Plans">
    Select **Subscription Plans** from the settings menu. You will see your current plan status and the available upgrade options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Tap **Choose Standard**, **Choose Medium**, or **Choose Pro** to select the tier you want. A summary of the plan's features and the monthly price appears before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay via KPay">
    You are redirected to the **KPay** secure checkout. Enter your card details and confirm the payment. Once KPay confirms the payment, Xera is notified automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan activates">
    Once KPay confirms the payment, your new plan activates immediately. Your verified badge appears on your profile and monetization features unlock (subject to the follower requirement).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  KPay accepts major debit and credit cards. The charge appears on your statement in USD at the monthly plan price. No partial refunds are issued for mid-cycle downgrades.
</Info>

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Subscription Plans → Manage Plan**. When you cancel:

- Your plan remains **active until the end of the current billing period**
- No further charges are made after cancellation
- Monetization features (tips, video revenue) stay on until the plan expires
- Your verified badge is removed when the plan period ends

<Tip>
  If you are approaching 1,000 followers and want to start earning as soon as you hit the threshold, upgrade to Medium or Pro now. Your badge activates immediately and monetization turns on automatically once the follower count is met — no extra steps required.
</Tip>
