Once you have at least **$5.00** in your available balance, you can request a withdrawal to your Mobile Money account. Xera supports the most widely used Mobile Money providers across Central and East Africa, and the process is straightforward: save your account details once, then submit withdrawal requests whenever your balance is ready. Payouts are processed manually by the Xera team and typically arrive within 2–5 business days.

## Supported Payout Methods

| Provider | Region | Typical processing time |
|---|---|---|
| **Airtel Money** | Central & East Africa | 2–5 business days |
| **Orange Money** | West & Central Africa | 2–5 business days |
| **M-Pesa** | East Africa | 2–5 business days |
| **Afrimoney** | West Africa | 2–5 business days |
| **Other (Mobile Money)** | Various | 2–5 business days |

<Info>
  All processing times are estimates. Actual transfer speed depends on your mobile operator and local network conditions.
</Info>

## Setting Up Your Payout Account

Configure your Mobile Money account before submitting your first withdrawal request. You only need to do this once — your details are saved securely and can be updated at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Creator Dashboard → Payout Settings">
    Open the **Creator Dashboard** from your profile menu. Scroll to the **Wallet & Withdrawals** section and locate the **Mobile Money Account** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Mobile Money provider">
    Click the **Provider** dropdown and choose the option that matches your account: Airtel Money, Orange Money, M-Pesa, Afrimoney, or Other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account holder name">
    Type the full name registered on the Mobile Money account in the **Account Holder** field (for example: `Jean-Paul Mbeki`). This name must match the account to avoid failed transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Mobile Money phone number">
    Enter your number in international format in the **Mobile Money Number** field. For example:

    ```
    +243810000000
    ```

    Include the country code prefix. Do not add spaces or dashes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your country code">
    Enter the two-letter ISO country code in the **Country** field (for example, `CD` for the Democratic Republic of Congo, `KE` for Kenya, `SN` for Senegal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the account">
    Click **Save Account**. A confirmation message appears when your payout settings are stored. You can return to update these details at any time by clicking **Edit** in the Mobile Money Account panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Double-check your phone number and account holder name before saving. If your payout account details are incorrect, your withdrawal request may be rejected and you will need to resubmit after correcting the settings.
</Warning>

## Requesting a Withdrawal

After your payout account is saved, follow these steps to request a transfer of your available earnings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your available balance is at least $5.00">
    Check the **Available for Withdrawal** card at the top of the Creator Dashboard. The amount displayed must be **$5.00 or more** to proceed. Pending earnings do not count toward the available balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the withdrawal amount">
    In the **Request a Withdrawal** panel, type the amount you want to withdraw in the **Amount (USD)** field. The field shows your current available balance for reference. The minimum withdrawal is **$5.00**.

    ```
    Minimum: $5.00
    Maximum: your full available balance
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit the request">
    Review the amount and confirm your saved payout account is displayed correctly. Click **Send Request** to submit. Xera registers your withdrawal request with a **Pending** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    The Xera team reviews and processes withdrawals manually. You will receive a push notification each time your withdrawal status changes. Processing typically takes **2–5 business days** from submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Withdrawal Statuses

After you submit a withdrawal request, it moves through the following states:

<Accordion title="Pending">
  Your request has been received and is in the queue for manual review. No funds have been moved yet. The amount is deducted from your available balance to prevent double-withdrawal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Processing">
  The Xera team has approved your request and is executing the Mobile Money transfer. You will receive a notification when this status is set.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Paid">
  The transfer was completed successfully. The amount is added to your **Total Paid Out** total. Check your Mobile Money balance to confirm receipt.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Rejected">
  The transfer could not be completed. Common reasons include an incorrect phone number, an unregistered Mobile Money account, or a network issue with your provider. Update your payout settings and resubmit your request.

  <Warning>
    If a withdrawal is rejected, the amount is returned to your available balance so you can request it again after correcting your account details.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

## API Reference

Use these endpoints to manage payout settings and withdrawal requests programmatically.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="POST /api/monetization/payout-settings">
    Saves or updates the Mobile Money account details for the authenticated creator.

    ```bash
    POST /api/monetization/payout-settings
    Authorization: Bearer <session_token>
    Content-Type: application/json
    ```

    ```json
    {
      "provider": "airtel_money",
      "account_name": "Jean-Paul Mbeki",
      "wallet_number": "+243810000000",
      "country_code": "CD",
      "notes": "Professional account"
    }
    ```

    **Body parameters:**

    <ParamField body="provider" type="string" required>
      Mobile Money provider identifier. One of: `airtel_money`, `orange_money`, `mpesa`, `afrimoney`, `other`.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="account_name" type="string" required>
      Full name of the Mobile Money account holder. Maximum 80 characters.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="wallet_number" type="string" required>
      Mobile Money phone number in international format (e.g. `+243810000000`). Maximum 32 characters.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="country_code" type="string">
      Two-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code (e.g. `CD`, `KE`). Defaults to `CD`.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="notes" type="string">
      Optional note for the Xera team (e.g. special transfer instructions). Maximum 280 characters.
    </ParamField>

    **Response:**

    <ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
      `true` when payout settings are saved successfully.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout_method" type="object">
      The saved payout method record including `provider`, `account_name`, `wallet_number`, `country_code`, and `updated_at`.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="POST /api/monetization/withdrawals">
    Creates a new withdrawal request for the authenticated creator.

    ```bash
    POST /api/monetization/withdrawals
    Authorization: Bearer <session_token>
    Content-Type: application/json
    ```

    ```json
    {
      "amount": 25.00
    }
    ```

    **Body parameters:**

    <ParamField body="amount" type="number" required>
      Withdrawal amount in USD. Must be at least `5.00` and no greater than the current available balance.
    </ParamField>

    **Response:**

    <ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">
      `true` when the withdrawal request is created.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="withdrawal" type="object">
      The new withdrawal record with `id`, `amount`, `status` (`pending`), `created_at`, and the payout method snapshot used.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="available_balance" type="number">
      Your updated available balance after the withdrawal amount has been reserved.
    </ResponseField>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Withdrawals are processed manually by the Xera team. You will receive a push notification to your device when your withdrawal status changes to **Processing** or **Paid**. Make sure push notifications are enabled in your browser or Xera PWA settings.
</Note>
