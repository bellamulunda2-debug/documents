Your Xera account is more than a login — it is the foundation of your public Trajectory. This guide walks you through every setting available in your account: shaping your profile identity, choosing a subscription plan that matches your goals, controlling how Xera notifies you, and keeping your credentials secure.

## Profile settings

Your profile is the first thing other builders see when they visit your Trajectory page. Keep it up to date to attract followers, collaborators, and opportunities.

To edit your profile, tap your **avatar** in the navigation bar and select **Settings → Profile**.

<ParamField body="Username" type="string" required>
  Your unique handle on Xera. It appears in your public URL (`xera.app/@username`) and cannot contain spaces. You can change it at any time, but doing so breaks existing links to your profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Display name" type="string" required>
  The name shown on your profile card and in the feed. Use your real name or the name you build under.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Bio" type="string">
  A short description of who you are and what you are building. Keep it specific — "Building a fintech app for freelancers" is more compelling than "Entrepreneur."
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Avatar" type="file">
  Upload a square image (JPEG or PNG recommended). Your avatar appears next to every Trace you publish, in the feed, and on live streams.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Area of focus" type="enum">
  Select the category that best describes your work. This influences how the discovery algorithm surfaces your profile to relevant audiences.

  Accepted values: `Developer`, `Creator`, `Entrepreneur`, `Student`, `Other`
</ParamField>

Tap **Save changes** after updating any field. Changes are reflected immediately across the platform.

## Subscription plans

Xera offers three paid plans on top of the free tier. Each plan unlocks a new level of visibility and earning potential. All plans are billed securely online and can be managed from **Settings → Subscription**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standard — $2.50 / mo">
    The Standard plan is the entry point for builders who want to signal commitment to the community.

    **What you unlock:**
    - A blue **verified badge** on your profile and every Trace you publish
    - Higher priority placement in the discovery feed
    - Verified status visible to recruiters and followers

    **Requirements:** None — available to all users.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Medium — $6.00 / mo">
    The Medium plan lets your audience support your work financially.

    **What you unlock:**
    - Everything in Standard, plus:
    - A **Soutenir (Support)** button on your profile so followers can send you tips
    - You receive **80 % of every tip** — Xera retains a 20 % platform commission
    - Mobile Money payout integration (Airtel Money, Orange Money, M-Pesa, Afrimoney)

    **Requirements:** You must have at least **1,000 followers** before the tip-receiving feature activates.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pro — $10.00 / mo">
    The Pro plan is for full-time builders who want to earn directly from their content and access advanced analytics.

    **What you unlock:**
    - Everything in Medium, plus:
    - **Video monetization** at **$0.40 per 1,000 views** on qualifying videos
    - A dedicated **Professional Page** — a business-facing profile for your brand or project
    - Advanced creator dashboard with revenue breakdown, payout history, and engagement analytics

    **Requirements:** At least **1,000 followers** for monetization features to activate. Videos must be **longer than 60 seconds** to count toward view-based revenue.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  To upgrade, go to **Settings → Subscription**, choose your plan, and complete the secure payment flow. Plan benefits activate as soon as your payment is confirmed. See [Subscription Plans](/monetization/subscription-plans) for a full feature comparison and annual billing options.
</Note>

### Professional Page (Pro only)

When you are on the Pro plan, Xera creates a dedicated **Professional Page** attached to your account. This page acts as a business-facing presence — separate from your personal builder profile — and is designed for your brand, project, or company. Recruiters browsing the **Elite Recruiter Portal** can discover and contact you through this page.

To configure your Professional Page, navigate to **Settings → Professional Page** and fill in your page name, description, and contact details.

## Notification preferences

Xera sends push notifications directly to your browser or installed PWA so you never miss a meaningful interaction. To manage what triggers a notification, go to **Settings → Notifications**.

<Accordion title="New followers">
  Receive a notification whenever someone follows your Trajectory. Staying aware of new followers helps you engage early — early engagement signals quality to the feed algorithm.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Trace likes">
  Get notified when another builder likes one of your Traces. You can toggle this per Trace type (text, image, video) if you want to reduce noise.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="New supporters (tips)">
  Receive an instant notification when a viewer sends you a tip through the **Soutenir** button or during a live stream. Available on Medium and Pro plans.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Live stream alerts">
  Get notified when a builder you follow goes live. You can limit these alerts to your top followed accounts to avoid interruptions.
</Accordion>

To enable browser push notifications for the first time, Xera will prompt you for permission. Click **Allow** in the browser dialog that appears. If you previously denied permission, go to your browser's site settings for `xera.app` and reset the notification permission, then reload the page.

<Tip>
  Install Xera as a PWA for the most reliable notification delivery. Open [xera.app](https://xera.app) in Chrome or Safari, tap the browser menu, and select **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install app"**. Notifications arrive even when your browser tab is closed.
</Tip>

## Account security

Keeping your account secure protects your Trajectory, your earnings, and your followers' trust.

### Change your email address

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Email**.
2. Enter your new email address and confirm it.
3. Xera sends a verification link to the new address.
4. Click the link in the email to confirm the change.

Your old address is immediately invalidated once you confirm.

### Change your password

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Password**.
2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
3. Click **Update password**.

Choose a password that is at least 12 characters long and unique to Xera.

### Sign out from all devices

If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, use the **Sign out all sessions** option:

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Security**.
2. Click **Sign out from all devices**.
3. Confirm the action.

All active sessions — including any device where you are currently logged in — are immediately invalidated. You will need to sign in again on each device.

<Warning>
  Signing out from all devices also terminates any active live stream you are broadcasting. Make sure you are not live before using this option.
</Warning>
