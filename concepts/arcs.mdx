An ARC is your mission — the structured project or goal that gives every trace you log a purpose and a home. Instead of posting updates into the void, you anchor your work to something concrete: a startup you're building, a skill you're mastering, a product you're shipping. ARCs turn scattered effort into visible, public proof of progression.

## What Is an ARC?

An ARC is a public project container. Think of it as a living document of your ambition: it holds your goal, tracks your milestones, and accumulates every trace you publish along the way. Recruiters, collaborators, and followers can follow your ARC directly — watching your progress in real time.

Every ARC has a **status** that reflects where you are:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | You're actively working on this ARC and logging traces |
| **Completed** | You've achieved the goal and closed the ARC |
| **Paused** | You've temporarily stopped work but haven't abandoned it |

## What an ARC Contains

Each ARC is built from several components that together tell the full story of your work:

- **Title** — A short, punchy name for your goal (e.g., *Launch Kira App*)
- **Description** — A brief explanation of what the project is and why it matters
- **Goal** — The specific outcome you're working toward
- **Milestones** — Discrete checkpoints that mark meaningful progress along the way
- **Trace History** — Every proof-of-work update you've logged under this ARC
- **Cover Media** — An optional image or video that represents the ARC visually
- **Stage** — Where the project is: Idea → Prototype → Demo → Beta → Release

## Creating an ARC

You can create an ARC in under a minute. Start from anywhere on Xera using the **+** button in the navigation bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap + Create">
    Press the **+** button in the bottom navigation bar or the top action bar. Select **ARC** from the content type menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Your Title and Description">
    Enter a clear, ambitious title (keep it under 60 characters — it appears on your public Trajectory). Add a description that explains what you're building and why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Goal">
    Write out the specific outcome you're aiming for. This keeps you accountable and tells your followers exactly what success looks like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Milestones">
    Break your goal into meaningful checkpoints. Add at least two or three milestones so your progress is trackable and visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Stage and Status">
    Select the current stage of your project (Idea, Prototype, Demo, Beta, or Release) and set the status to **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Tap **Publish**. Your ARC is now live on your public profile and visible to the Xera community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working with Milestones

Milestones are the checkpoints inside your ARC that signal real progress. They keep you honest about what "done" looks like at each stage, and they give your followers something concrete to cheer for.

When you create or edit an ARC, add milestones directly in the milestone section. Each milestone has:

- A **title** describing what you need to accomplish
- An optional **description** with more detail
- A **completion status** you update as you hit it

Mark a milestone complete from your ARC detail page whenever you reach it. Completing milestones updates your **Progression** score in your Trajectory dashboard.

## ARC Visibility

By default, all ARCs are **public** — anyone on Xera (and on the web) can view your ARC and follow your progress. This is intentional: the whole point of Xera is that your effort is visible.

<Info>
  If you want to keep an ARC private while you're in early stages, you can adjust visibility in the ARC settings after creation. Private ARCs still log traces but do not appear in the discovery feed or on your public Trajectory.
</Info>

## Managing Your ARCs

You can update any ARC from your profile or directly from the ARC detail page. Tap the **⋯** menu on any ARC card to access the following options:

<Accordion title="Edit">
  Update the title, description, goal, milestones, cover image, or stage at any time. Changes take effect immediately on your public profile.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pause">
  Set the status to **Paused** when you need to step back temporarily. Paused ARCs remain on your profile but are marked clearly so followers know you've stepped back.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Complete">
  Mark an ARC as **Completed** once you've achieved your goal. Completing an ARC boosts your Trajectory's ARC Completion Rate metric.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Archive">
  Archive an ARC to remove it from your active view without deleting it. Archived ARCs and their trace history are preserved and still accessible via direct link.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Delete">
  Permanently delete an ARC and all associated traces. This action cannot be undone.
  <Warning>Deleting an ARC removes all traces linked to it. Export your trace history before deleting if you want to keep a record.</Warning>
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Keep your ARC titles short and ambitious. Titles appear on your public Trajectory timeline and in the discovery feed — they're often the first thing a recruiter or collaborator reads about you.
</Tip>
