Your Trajectory is Xera's visual proof that you ship. Every trace you publish feeds it, every milestone you hit advances it, and every day you show up strengthens it. While other platforms measure your worth in likes and follower counts, your Trajectory measures the only thing that actually matters: are you building, consistently, over time?

## What the Trajectory Shows

Your Trajectory dashboard pulls together every signal of your activity on Xera into a single view. Open it from your profile to see:

- **Consistency Heatmap** — A GitHub-style grid showing every day you logged a trace, colored by activity intensity. The fuller the grid, the stronger your streak.
- **Activity Graph** — A timeline chart of your trace volume and engagement over the past 30, 90, or 365 days.
- **Momentum Score** — A real-time score (0–100) that reflects your recent activity velocity.
- **ARC Completion Rate** — The percentage of ARC milestones you've marked complete across all your active and completed ARCs.
- **Engagement Stats** — Likes, comments, views, and shares your traces have received, broken down by ARC and time period.
- **Follower Growth** — How your audience has grown over time, with a chart showing week-by-week movement.

## Trajectory Metrics Explained

Your Trajectory is powered by four core metrics. Understanding them helps you know exactly what to focus on to grow.

<Accordion title="Consistency">
  Consistency measures how regularly you log traces over time. It rewards showing up every day — or close to it — rather than posting a burst of updates and going silent. A stable, low-variance posting cadence scores higher than sporadic spikes.

  **How to improve it:** Log a trace every day, even a short one. Daily updates — however small — compound into a strong consistency score faster than any other action.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Momentum">
  Momentum is your recent activity velocity. It weights your last 14–30 days more heavily than your full history, so it rises quickly when you're active and falls when you go quiet. Think of it as a "are you building right now?" signal.

  The Momentum score (0–100) is calculated from:
  - Frequency of traces (how many days out of the period you logged)
  - Intensity (how much activity per day)
  - Consistency (how evenly distributed that activity was)

  **How to improve it:** Get back to daily logging. Momentum recovers fast once you restart your streak.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Progression">
  Progression is the percentage of ARC milestones you've completed. It's a direct measure of how much of your stated goals you've actually achieved — not just attempted.

  **How to improve it:** Break your ARCs into smaller, achievable milestones, then mark them complete as you hit them. Completing an ARC counts as 100% progression for that project and boosts your overall rate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Engagement">
  Engagement captures how other builders and followers interact with your traces: views, likes, comments, shares, and time spent reading. It's a signal of the quality and relevance of your work, not just its quantity.

  **How to improve it:** Post specific, detailed traces with real proof (screenshots, metrics, demos). The more concrete your updates, the more they resonate with others.
</Accordion>

## How Your Trajectory Affects Your Feed Ranking

Your Trajectory isn't just a personal dashboard — it directly affects how visible you are across Xera. The recommendation algorithm uses a composite score to rank content in the discovery feed:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Engagement (views, likes, shares, comments) | 40% |
| Creator Quality (followers, badge, monetization tier) | 30% |
| Freshness (recency of your last trace) | 15% |
| Retention (return visitors, watch time) | 10% |
| Virality (share rate, growth velocity) | 5% |

Beyond the composite score, the feed applies a distribution split:

- **65%** of feed slots go to verified builders (Standard plan and above)
- **35%** go to organic discovery (all active builders)

This means maintaining a strong Trajectory — regular traces, completed milestones, and genuine engagement — directly increases how often your work surfaces to new audiences.

<Note>
  The verified builder badge (Standard plan at $2.50/month) unlocks the 65% feed distribution priority. If you're building seriously on Xera, verification is the single highest-leverage action you can take for visibility.
</Note>

## Sharing Your Trajectory

Your Trajectory is public by default. Anyone can view it at your profile URL:

```
https://xera.app/profile?id=YOUR_USER_ID
```

Share this link anywhere you want to show proof of your work:

- In your GitHub profile README
- In your LinkedIn "About" section or featured links
- In a job application or portfolio
- In a cold outreach email to potential collaborators

Elite recruiters on Xera also have direct access to search and filter builder Trajectories — so a strong public profile actively works for you even when you're not promoting it.

<Tip>
  A strong Trajectory is your best resume. It shows exactly what you've built, how long you've been at it, and whether you show up consistently. Link it from your LinkedIn and GitHub — it says more in one glance than any CV ever could.
</Tip>
