Once your Elite Access is active, the talent search engine lets you find builders who match your exact requirements. Unlike keyword-matching on a resume database, Xera's search surfaces candidates based on what they have actually built and how consistently they show up — so every result is a builder who has demonstrated their skills in public.

## Accessing Talent Search

Log in to your Xera account and open the **Explorer** tab on your Pro Page. This tab is locked until you have an active Elite subscription. Once unlocked, it gives you full access to the certified builder database and all Elite filters.

<Note>
If the Explorer tab is not visible after subscribing, refresh your page or log out and back in. Your Elite access activates immediately upon payment confirmation.
</Note>

## Search Filters

Use the following filters individually or in combination to narrow results to exactly the builders you need.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Domain / Skill Area" icon="code">
    Filter by the technical or creative domain a builder works in. Examples include **JavaScript**, **AI / Machine Learning**, **Product Design**, **Marketing**, **DevOps**, and more. Domains are derived from the ARC topics a builder has committed to and completed — not self-reported tags.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Activity Level" icon="chart-line">
    Filter by how recently a builder has posted Traces:
    - **Active in last 7 days** — builders who logged work this week.
    - **Active in last 30 days** — builders who have been consistently active this month.

    Use the 7-day filter when you need someone ready to start immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Certification Status" icon="badge-check">
    On Elite, the **Certified only** filter is on by default. Certified profiles are builders whose Trajectory activity has been verified as consistent by the Xera algorithm. You can disable this filter to broaden results, but certified profiles represent the highest-signal candidates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Momentum Score" icon="bolt">
    Filter by Trajectory strength — from emerging builders to elite-tier shippers. The Momentum score is calculated algorithmically from posting frequency, consistency, and ARC completion rate. A high Momentum score means the builder shows up regularly, not just in bursts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Understanding the Momentum Engine

The Momentum Engine is the ranking system that powers the Explorer search results. It ranks builder profiles by the quality and consistency of their recent activity — not by follower count, endorsements, or connections.

The engine weighs four key signals:

| Signal | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Recent activity** | Traces posted in the last 7–30 days |
| **Consistency** | How regularly the builder ships (daily/weekly patterns) |
| **ARC completion** | Percentage of committed projects completed |
| **Engagement** | Community responses to their Traces and ARCs |

A builder who posts one Trace every single day scores higher on consistency than someone who posts thirty times in a week and goes silent. The Momentum Engine is designed to surface builders who are reliable over time — not just impressive in a sprint.

<Info>
The Momentum Engine does not factor in follower count or how long a builder has been on Xera. A builder who joined last month and ships daily can outrank someone with years of history who has gone quiet.
</Info>

## Reading a Builder Profile

When you open a builder's profile from the Explorer, here is what each section tells you:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trajectory Timeline" icon="timeline">
    The full history of the builder's ARCs and Traces, plotted visually over time. Use this to assess the shape of their career — steady growth, recent acceleration, or gaps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active ARCs" icon="folder-open">
    The projects and goals they are currently building. Active ARCs show you what they are focused on right now and how aligned that work is with your needs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consistency Score" icon="calendar-days">
    A percentage reflecting how regularly they log Traces relative to their stated ARC commitments. Higher consistency means they follow through on what they start.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skills & Domains" icon="tag">
    Derived automatically from their ARC topics — not self-reported. These tags reflect the domains where the builder has actually shipped work, not just interests they listed on signup.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Contacting a Builder

When you find a builder you want to reach, click the **Contact** button on their profile. This opens a direct message thread between you and the builder inside Xera's messaging system. There is no limit on how many builders you can contact with an Elite plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a builder's profile">
    Click any result in the Explorer tab to view their full profile, Trajectory, and active ARCs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review their work history">
    Check their Trajectory timeline, consistency score, and ARC completion rate before reaching out. The more context you have, the more relevant your message will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Contact">
    Click the **Contact** button to open a direct message. Write a personalised message referencing the specific ARCs or skills that caught your attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Filter by **domain** first, then sort by **Momentum score** to find builders who are actively shipping in your area of need — not just ones with an impressive-looking profile that has been dormant for months. The combination of a high Momentum score and recent 7-day activity is the strongest signal of a candidate ready to engage.
</Tip>

<Note>
**What "certified" means on Xera:** a certified profile is not a manual verification. It is assigned by the Xera algorithm based on consistent, sustained Trajectory activity over time. Builders earn certification by showing up — and they lose it if they go inactive for an extended period.
</Note>
