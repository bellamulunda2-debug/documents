## Why Xera Is Different for Hiring

Most hiring platforms show you a static resume. Xera shows you a living record of work. When a builder joins Xera, their profile grows over time through three core layers:

- **ARCs** — structured projects and goals with defined milestones, publicly committed to and tracked.
- **Traces** — daily proof-of-work logs tied to each ARC, showing consistency and velocity over time.
- **Trajectory** — a visual evolution dashboard that plots the full arc of a builder's progress.

A certified builder profile on Xera means the algorithm has verified consistent Trajectory activity. You are not looking at someone who claimed skills — you are looking at someone who demonstrated them, day after day, in public.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the plans page">
    Go to [xera.app/premium-plans](https://xera.app/premium-plans). You must be signed in to your Xera account to proceed.

  <Step title="Choose your billing cycle">
    Select **Monthly** ($40/mo) for a no-commitment option, or **Annual** ($384/yr) to save $96 and unlock the Gold Recruiter badge plus quarterly trend reports.

  <Step title="Complete checkout via KPay">
    You will be redirected to the KPay checkout. Enter your card details to complete the payment. Xera processes all payments in CDF through the KPay gateway.

  <Step title="Access the Explorer tab">

  </Step>
</Steps>
