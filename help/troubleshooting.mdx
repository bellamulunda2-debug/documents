If something is not working as expected on Xera, this guide covers the most common issues and the fastest way to resolve them. Work through the steps for your specific issue and, if the problem persists, the support section at the bottom explains how to reach the team.

## Login & Account Access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't log in">
    If you cannot sign in to your account, work through these steps in order:

    1. **Check your credentials** — confirm you are using the correct email address and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    2. **Reset your password** — on the login page, click **Forgot password?** and follow the instructions sent to your email. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive within a couple of minutes.
    3. **Clear browser cache and cookies** — stale session data can block new logins. In your browser settings, clear cookies and cached data for `xera.app`, then try again.
    4. **Try a different browser or device** — if the issue is browser-specific, this will confirm it. Chrome and Edge are recommended for the best Xera experience.

    <Tip>
      If you signed up using Google or GitHub, make sure you are clicking **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** on the login page rather than entering a password — those accounts do not have a separate Xera password.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account not verified">
    After registering with email, Xera sends a verification link to the address you provided. If your account is not verified:

    1. **Check your spam or junk folder** — verification emails are sometimes filtered by mail providers.
    2. **Search for the sender** — look for an email from `hello@xera.app` or `team@xera.app`.
    3. **Request a new link** — on the login screen, click **Resend verification email** and enter your address. A fresh link will be sent immediately.
    4. **Ensure the link has not expired** — verification links are time-limited. If yours has expired, simply request a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Logged out unexpectedly">
    Xera uses session-based authentication, and sessions expire after a period of inactivity to keep your account secure. If you were logged out without warning:

    1. **Log in again** — go to [xera.app](https://xera.app) and sign in with your credentials.
    2. **Enable "Remember me"** — check the **Remember me** option on the login screen to extend session persistence across browser restarts.
    3. **Avoid using private / incognito mode** as a primary browser — these modes discard session cookies when the window closes, causing automatic logouts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Feed & Discovery

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My Traces aren't appearing in the feed">
    If your Traces are not showing up in the discovery feed, check the following:

    1. **New accounts require an indexing period** — if your account is less than 24 hours old, give the algorithm time to establish a baseline score for your profile.
    2. **Confirm your profile is public** — go to **Settings → Privacy** and ensure your profile visibility is set to **Public**. Private profiles do not appear in any feed.
    3. **Publish at least 3 Traces** — the algorithm needs enough content to calculate a meaningful score before surfacing your profile to other users.
    4. **Check ARC visibility** — if the ARC linked to your Traces is set to Private, those Traces will not appear in public feeds regardless of your profile setting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Feed shows the same creators repeatedly">
    The recommendation algorithm learns from your engagement history. If it seems repetitive:

    1. **Engage with new content** — like, comment on, or watch Traces from builders you have not interacted with before. The algorithm interprets this as a signal to broaden your recommendations.
    2. **Follow more diverse builders** — follow builders in different fields or at different stages of their projects to expand the diversity pool in your feed.
    3. **Be patient** — engagement affinity data accumulates over time. The feed becomes noticeably more varied after a week or two of regular use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recommendation score feels low">
    Your composite score is calculated from five signals. Here is how to move each one in the right direction:

    | Signal | How to improve it |
    |---|---|
    | **Engagement (40%)** | Publish content that drives views, completions, and tips |
    | **Creator Quality (30%)** | Grow your followers, get verified, upgrade your plan |
    | **Freshness (15%)** | Publish Traces consistently — even short ones count |
    | **Retention (10%)** | Create video Traces that viewers watch all the way through |
    | **Virality (5%)** | Produce shareable milestones and notable project updates |

    Completing ARC milestones tends to generate engagement spikes that lift your score significantly in a short time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Monetization & Payouts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Monetization is locked">
    Monetization requires both of the following at the same time:

    - **1,000 or more followers** on your Xera profile
    - **An active Medium ($6.00/mo) or Pro ($10.00/mo) subscription**

    Check your current follower count in your profile header and your subscription status in **Settings → Subscription**. If you meet both requirements and monetization is still locked, log out and log back in to refresh your session, then check again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Withdrawal rejected">
    A rejected withdrawal is almost always caused by one of these issues — fix the relevant one and resubmit:

    1. **Incorrect phone number format** — your Mobile Money number must be entered in international format, for example `+243810000000`. Go to **Creator Dashboard → Payout Settings**, correct the number, and save before resubmitting.
    2. **Insufficient balance** — confirm your available balance is at least **$5.00**. Your balance may have dipped below the minimum between submission and processing if earnings were still settling.
    3. **Inactive Mobile Money account** — verify with your mobile operator that the phone number is active and able to receive transfers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Video revenue not counting">
    Video earnings only accumulate for videos that are **longer than 60 seconds** and have been processed by the platform. If a video is not generating revenue:

    1. **Check video length** — videos 60 seconds or shorter are not eligible for view monetization, regardless of view count.
    2. **Wait 24 hours** — video view data goes through a processing pipeline before being credited. Revenue for a newly uploaded video may not appear immediately in your dashboard.
    3. **Confirm Pro plan is active** — video monetization ($0.40 per 1,000 views) is exclusive to the Pro tier. If you are on Medium, tip revenue is available but video view revenue is not.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tip not received">
    Tips sent by supporters (or through live stream "Soutenir" actions) do not credit instantly. They go through a short holding period before they appear as available balance in your Creator Dashboard. If a tip was sent but you do not see it yet:

    1. **Wait for settlement** — check your dashboard again after a short period; the tip should appear once the holding window closes.
    2. **Check your transaction history** — go to **Creator Dashboard → Transactions** to see whether the tip is listed as *Pending* or *Credited*.
    3. **Confirm monetization is active** — if monetization was disabled at the time the tip was sent, the transaction may not have been processed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Live Streaming

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stream won't start">
    If your live stream fails to launch, follow these steps:

    1. **Check browser permissions** — click the lock icon in your browser's address bar and ensure both **Camera** and **Microphone** are set to **Allow** for `xera.app`.
    2. **Use Chrome or Edge** — these browsers provide the best WebRTC compatibility for Xera live streams. Firefox and Safari may have limitations.
    3. **Close other apps using your camera** — video conferencing tools, other browser tabs, or camera apps can block access. Close them and refresh the page.
    4. **Restart your browser** — if permissions were recently changed, a full browser restart is sometimes needed for the change to take effect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Viewers can't hear audio">
    Audio is muted by default when a viewer first joins a stream to comply with browser autoplay policies. Viewers should:

    1. **Click the unmute button** — labelled **"Activer le son"** in the stream player controls.
    2. **Check system volume** — confirm the device is not muted at the OS level and that the browser tab is not muted (right-click the tab to check).
    3. **Refresh the stream page** — if the audio track dropped, a refresh re-establishes the connection.

    As the streamer, if your own audio is not being picked up: confirm your microphone is selected as the correct input device in your OS audio settings and that it is not physically muted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Chat not loading">
    If the live chat panel is blank or not updating:

    1. **Refresh the stream page** — a simple reload re-establishes the real-time chat connection in most cases.
    2. **Check your internet connection** — real-time chat requires a stable connection. Switch to a different network or disable a VPN if you are using one.
    3. **Disable browser extensions** — ad blockers or privacy extensions can interfere with WebSocket connections. Try the stream in a private/incognito window to test.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscriptions & Payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment failed">
    If your subscription payment did not go through:

    1. **Check your KPay balance** — ensure your KPay account has sufficient funds to cover the subscription amount.
    2. **Verify your payment details** — if you are paying by card, confirm the card number, expiry date, and CVV are all correct.
    3. **Try a different payment method** — KPay supports multiple funding sources. Switch to a different method and try again.
    4. **Contact KPay support** — if your account is restricted or your payment is being declined by KPay's fraud filters, reach out to KPay directly to resolve the block.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Subscription not activating after payment">
    Payment confirmation and subscription activation can take a few minutes while your payment is being verified.

    1. **Wait up to 10 minutes** — in most cases the subscription activates automatically once your payment is confirmed as completed.
    2. **Refresh the page** — after waiting, reload **Settings → Subscription** to see the updated plan status.
    3. **Log out and log back in** — this forces a session refresh that pulls your latest plan data.
    4. **Contact support** — if more than 10 minutes have passed and your plan is still showing as inactive, reach out via the in-app messaging with your payment reference number so the team can confirm activation manually.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upgraded plan benefits not showing">
    After a successful plan upgrade, some benefits (advanced dashboard panels, monetization toggles, verified badge) are tied to your active session. If they are not visible:

    1. **Log out and log back in** — this is the fastest fix. Your session needs to refresh to pick up the new plan permissions.
    2. **Clear your browser cache** — cached pages can show stale UI. Clear the cache for `xera.app` and reload.
    3. **Check your plan status** — go to **Settings → Subscription** and confirm the plan shows as **Active**, not *Pending* or *Past Due*.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## PWA & Browser

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Offline mode not working">
    Xera's PWA includes a service worker that caches key resources for offline access. If offline mode is not working:

    1. **Install the PWA properly** — offline support only works after you install Xera using the browser's **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"** prompt. Simply visiting the site in a browser tab does not enable full offline capability.
    2. **Load the app while online first** — the service worker needs at least one successful online session to cache resources before offline mode is available.
    3. **Note feature limitations** — posting new Traces, loading fresh feed content, and real-time chat all require an active internet connection. Offline mode covers cached pages and previously loaded content only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Push notifications not arriving">
    If you are not receiving Xera push notifications on your device:

    1. **Check browser notification permissions** — in your browser settings, navigate to **Site Settings → Notifications** and confirm that notifications for `xera.app` are set to **Allow**, not *Block* or *Ask*.
    2. **Check OS-level permissions** — on mobile and desktop, your operating system also controls notification delivery. Go to your device's notification settings and ensure your browser app is allowed to display notifications.
    3. **Re-subscribe in Xera** — go to **Settings → Notifications** in Xera and toggle push notifications off, then back on. This re-registers your browser subscription.
    4. **Check Do Not Disturb** — notifications are silenced when Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is active on your device.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
