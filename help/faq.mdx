Whether you are just getting started or have been building on Xera for a while, you will find answers to the most common questions below. Topics are grouped by category — jump to the section that fits your situation and expand any question to read the full answer.

## Account & Profile

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my username?">
    Go to your profile page and tap the **Edit Profile** button. You can update your username from the profile settings form. Keep in mind that your username is part of your public URL (`xera.app/@username`), so any change will immediately update all links pointing to your profile. Choose carefully — frequent changes can confuse followers who know you by your current handle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple profiles?">
    No. Each Xera account is tied to a single verified identity. You can only have one profile per email address. This is intentional — Xera is built around authentic, verifiable trajectories, so multiple profiles under the same person would undermine the proof-of-work record you are building.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Navigate to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. You will be asked to confirm your decision. Account deletion is permanent: all your ARCs, Traces, Trajectory data, followers, and earnings history will be removed and cannot be recovered. If you have a pending withdrawal balance, make sure to request a payout before deleting your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Xera available as a mobile app?">
    Xera is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it directly from your browser on any device — no app store required. On mobile, open Xera in Chrome or Safari and look for the **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"** prompt in your browser menu. Once installed, Xera behaves like a native app with a home screen icon and full-screen experience.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## ARCs & Traces

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many ARCs can I have active at once?">
    You can run multiple ARCs at the same time — there is no hard cap. However, the quality of your Trajectory is directly linked to how consistently you log Traces. Having too many active ARCs spread across too many goals can dilute your proof-of-work record. Most builders find that focusing on two or three ARCs at a time produces a stronger, more coherent Trajectory.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I make an ARC private?">
    Yes. When you create or edit an ARC, you can set its visibility to **Private**. A private ARC is only visible to you — it will not appear on your public Trajectory, in the discovery feed, or to your followers. You can switch an ARC from private to public at any time to make your accumulated progress visible retroactively.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a Trace after publishing?">
    You can edit the text and metadata of a Trace after publishing. However, the original timestamp is locked — it cannot be changed — to preserve the integrity of your proof-of-work record. Deleting a Trace is also possible, but doing so permanently removes that entry from your Trajectory. Deleted Traces are not recoverable and will reduce your logged consistency for that period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does a Trace affect my Trajectory?">
    Every Trace you publish is a timestamped data point on your public Trajectory. Your Trajectory dashboard calculates your consistency streak (consecutive days with a published Trace), your momentum (rate of progress on active ARCs), and your overall activity timeline. Regular Traces — especially those with rich content like videos, metrics, or milestone completions — produce a stronger, denser Trajectory that is more compelling to followers and recruiters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Discovery Feed

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see my Traces in the feed?">
    New accounts need a short indexing period before they appear in other users' discovery feeds. Make sure your profile is set to **Public** and that you have published at least a few Traces to establish a presence. If you have just created your account, give it 24 hours and publish consistently — the algorithm begins surfacing your content once it has enough signal to rank you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the recommendation algorithm work?">
    Xera's feed algorithm ranks builders using a composite score built from five weighted signals:

    | Signal | Weight |
    |---|---|
    | **Engagement** (views, completions, tips, comments) | 40% |
    | **Creator Quality** (followers, verification, plan tier) | 30% |
    | **Freshness** (how recently you published) | 15% |
    | **Retention** (return visitors, average watch time) | 10% |
    | **Virality** (view growth rate, shares) | 5% |

    Your feed is also split between **65% verified builders** and **35% organic discovery**, so verified accounts with active Trajectories receive meaningfully more exposure. The more consistently you publish quality Traces, the higher your composite score climbs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get more visibility on Xera?">
    The most reliable path to visibility is consistent, quality output. Here are the highest-impact actions:

    - **Publish Traces regularly** — freshness is weighted at 15% and directly affects how often you appear in feeds
    - **Complete ARC milestones** — milestone completions generate engagement spikes that boost your score
    - **Upgrade to Standard or higher** — verified builders receive a significant placement advantage (65% of the feed is reserved for them)
    - **Add video to your Traces** — video content drives higher completion rates, which feed directly into your engagement score
    - **Engage with other builders** — comments and interactions build affinity signals that improve cross-profile recommendations
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Monetization & Payouts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I activate monetization?">
    To activate monetization you must meet two requirements simultaneously:

    1. **Reach 1,000 followers** on your Xera profile
    2. **Hold an active Medium ($6.00/mo) or Pro ($10.00/mo) subscription**

    Once both conditions are met, go to your **Creator Dashboard → Monetization** and toggle monetization on. You will also need to save a payout method (Airtel Money, Orange Money, M-Pesa, Afrimoney, or another Mobile Money provider) before you can receive or withdraw earnings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do withdrawals take?">
    Withdrawal requests are processed within **2–5 business days**. After you submit a withdrawal, the request moves through a review stage before the funds are dispatched to your Mobile Money account. You can track the status of any withdrawal — *Pending*, *Processing*, or *Paid* — directly from your Creator Dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the minimum withdrawal amount?">
    The minimum withdrawal amount is **$5.00**. Requests for less than this amount will not be accepted. If your available balance is below the threshold, continue earning through supporter tips, video views, or live stream tips until you reach the minimum.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my payout rejected?">
    A payout is typically rejected for one of two reasons:

    - **Incorrect account information** — the phone number format you entered for your Mobile Money account is wrong or the account is not active. Use international format (e.g., `+243810000000`) when saving your payout settings.
    - **Insufficient balance** — your available balance fell below the $5.00 minimum between the time you submitted and the time it was processed.

    Correct the issue in **Creator Dashboard → Payout Settings** and resubmit your withdrawal request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Xera take a commission?">
    Yes. Xera retains a **20% platform commission** on all transactions — including supporter tips, live stream tips ("Soutenir"), and video view payouts. You receive **80% net** of every transaction. This commission is calculated automatically; the amount shown in your Creator Dashboard already reflects your net earnings after the commission is applied.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscriptions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription anytime?">
    Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Subscription → Manage Plan**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain all plan benefits until that date. After cancellation, your account reverts to the Free tier and monetization features are paused if they were active under a paid plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my subscription expires?">
    When your subscription lapses — whether due to cancellation or a failed payment — your account drops to the Free tier. Your verified badge is removed, your monetization features are paused, and new earnings will not accumulate until you reactivate a qualifying plan. Your existing balance, ARCs, Traces, and Trajectory data are all preserved and will be fully accessible again once you renew.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade from Standard to Pro?">
    Go to **Settings → Subscription → Change Plan** and select **Pro ($10.00/mo)**. You will be directed to the KPay checkout to complete the payment. The upgrade takes effect as soon as the payment is confirmed — usually within a few minutes. After upgrading, log out and log back in if your new plan benefits (advanced dashboard, video monetization) are not immediately visible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
